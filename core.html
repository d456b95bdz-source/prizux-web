The Decision without any noise.

Most conventional market A.I. systems attempt to predict the exact future price of an asset. This approach assumes that price movement is a stable, learnable target.

In reality, price itself is the noisiest signal in the market. Short-term randomness, external events, and micro-structure effects severely degrade model reliability when exact values are treated as truth.

PRIZUX Core takes a fundamentally different approach.

Instead of predicting a precise price, AI-1 evaluates probabilistic tendencies across multiple time horizons simultaneously. This allows the system to suppress unstable fluctuations and focus on directional structure rather than numerical precision.

Historical data accumulation for AI-1 begins in early 2018, covering multiple volatility regimes and market cycles.

In internal validation, this probabilistic multi-horizon approach demonstrated a 18.7% improvement in signal consistency compared to earlier single-path inference structures.
